# AutoCare Brand

## Product
Garage operations and customer portal for Vietnamese vehicle service.

## Audience
Receptionists need fast, error-resistant operational screens. Customers need clear appointment status and one primary action.

## Voice
Calm, utilitarian, trustworthy. Vietnamese plain language. Never promise an unverified outcome.

## Existing visual system
- Preserve Geist Sans and Geist Mono.
- Preserve semantic tokens from `src/app/globals.css`.
- Dense staff lists; roomy customer actions; cards support grouping, not decoration.
- Accessible focus rings and status labels remain mandatory.
